# Discord accounts are getting hacked. Here's what I'd do.

A lot of people in the community are getting their Discord accounts hacked right now. You've probably seen the DMs asking you to try a game or download a Minecraft mod. I’ll keep this quick. Here’s what you can do **now** so you’re not screwed if it happens, and what to do if it already did.

## Do this now

- [ ] **Lock down your email and Discord.** Use a different password for each and turn on MFA for both. Save your Discord backup codes somewhere you can get to if your PC or phone is out of the picture. [Discord MFA setup](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/219576828-Setting-up-Multi-Factor-Authentication)
- [ ] **Check Discord’s Devices and Authorized Apps.** Sign out sessions you don’t recognize and remove apps you don’t trust. MFA still matters, but a stealer can grab a session that’s already signed in. [Discord account security](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/24160905919511-My-Discord-Account-was-Hacked-or-Compromised)
- [ ] **Back up what you’d miss.** Request your Discord data package for a copy of your available data. If you run a server, make a template for your channels and roles, save important posts separately, and make sure you can reach a mod outside Discord. The export and template are copies for reference or rebuilding. They can’t recover your account or restore chat history. [Data package](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004957991-Your-Discord-Data-Package) · [Server templates](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041033511-Server-Templates)
- [ ] **For now, skip downloads sent through Discord DMs, even from friends.** That goes for any file or download link, whether it’s a Minecraft mod, a game, or something unrelated. A friend’s account can be hacked. If you need a public download, go to the developer’s official page yourself. If you’re expecting a personal file, text or call your friend and ask them to share it another way.

## What I'd use

- **Passwords:** I’d use [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/tools-and-features/) if you switch between platforms. If you already use Apple’s built-in Passwords app, you can use those same passwords on a Windows PC in Chrome. Install [iCloud for Windows and enable the iCloud Passwords extension](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud-windows/set-up-icloud-passwords-icw2babf5e03/icloud). Either way, let the manager generate a different password for every account and turn on MFA for the account that protects your vault.
- **Antivirus:** Windows Defender is already built in. [Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/consumer/antivirus) is an option if you want a dedicated real-time antivirus. Pick one real-time product. [Malwarebytes Free](https://www.malwarebytes.com/solutions/virus-scanner) is good for a second opinion when you run a scan manually.
- **Email:** I’d use [Proton Mail](https://proton.me/mail) for a private address for account logins and recovery. Turn on MFA and save your recovery details somewhere safe. Keep that address off public profiles and use a separate address for business inquiries or aliases for random signups.
- **Breach check:** Run your email addresses through [Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com/). If one shows up, check what was exposed. If a password was leaked, change it on that site and anywhere you reused it. I'd move important accounts off an address that's widely exposed or getting hit with targeted phishing. Keep access to the old inbox until you've verified that your logins and recovery emails work at the new address. A breach hit alone tells you your address appeared in a leak. It doesn't tell you someone can sign into your inbox.

## If you already ran it or got hacked

1. **Get on a trusted device.** If you ran the file, disconnect that PC from Wi-Fi or Ethernet. Use your phone or another trusted device to check your email and recover your accounts. If your email may be compromised too, change its password and sign out other sessions.
2. **If your Discord email changed, act fast.** Check your original inbox for Discord’s recovery email. The link expires after **48 hours** and starts with `https://discord.com/wasntme/`. After using it, set MFA back up and re-add your phone number if you had one. [Discord email-change recovery](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/27299669812375-My-Discord-Email-was-Changed-and-I-Want-to-Undo-It)
3. **If your password works but someone else added MFA, check your options.** A friend hit this exact problem. Their email stayed the same, so there was no email-change link. Check for an existing session on a trusted device and leave it open. At login, pick **Verify with something else** and try a saved, unused backup code or SMS if you’d already set it up. If neither works, [file a hacked-account ticket](https://dis.gd/hackedaccount) and explain that your password works, someone else added MFA, and your email stayed the same. Discord says support can’t remove MFA or issue new backup codes if you don’t have your codes, so recovery’s uncertain. [Discord MFA lockout guide](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001221072-How-to-Recover-Your-Discord-Account-When-Locked-Out-of-Multi-Factor-Authentication-MFA)
4. **If you can still sign in, lock down the account.** Reset your Discord password from the trusted device, sign out other sessions, and check your email, phone, MFA, and Authorized Apps. Replace exposed backup codes. If you’re locked out and the email recovery link is missing, expired, or fails, use the [hacked-account form](https://dis.gd/hackedaccount). Warn friends and mods somewhere outside Discord so they don’t open anything sent from your account.
5. **Deal with the PC and your other logins.** From a trusted device, change exposed or reused passwords and sign out other sessions where you can. Start with email, your password manager, and financial accounts. On Windows, update Defender and run a full scan and an offline scan. Understand that a clean scan can’t prove the PC is clean or take back what was already stolen. For the strongest assurance after running a suspected stealer, back up irreplaceable personal files and reinstall Windows from official media made on a trusted PC. Leave the suspicious download, unknown `.jar` or executable files, old installers, and old browser profiles and extensions out of the restore.
